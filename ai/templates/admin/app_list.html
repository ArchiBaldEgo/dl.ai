It feeds the LEFT navigation sidebar (#nav-sidebar, rendered by admin/nav_sidebar.html on every admin page) and the dashboard. Goals (per ТЗ): a convenient, tidy, ordered left nav. * AI tool groups injected by AIAdminSite.each_context have an app_label starting with "ai-tools" (see site.py::_build_ai_nav_apps). Their per-model rows keep the stock markup/classes so the stock nav-filter JS, `current-app`/`current-model` highlight and CSS all keep working.
